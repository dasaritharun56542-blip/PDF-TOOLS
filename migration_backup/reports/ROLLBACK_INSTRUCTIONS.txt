========================================
ROLLBACK INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

To revert to original SQLite databases:

1. Restore settings_before_postgresql.py to config/settings.py:
   copy migration_backup\reports\settings_before_postgresql.py config\settings.py

2. The original db.sqlite3 and media_db.sqlite3 files remain untouched in the project root and in migration_backup\database\.

3. Restart the Django development server.
